PunchWatch Privacy Statement

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Hey there, Fighter!

I'm George Yameen, the creator of PunchWatch. I built this app because I love boxing and wanted a better way to track my punches. Here's the straight talk on how your data is handled.

What PunchWatch Does & Doesn't Do

PunchWatch uses your Apple Watch sensors to track your punches and provide feedback. Your detailed punch data stays on your device. Only a short workout summary is sent, see Analytics below. We collect anonymous usage analytics to improve the app.

Your Data Stays Put

PunchWatch processes your punch tracking locally on your Apple Watch and iPhone. Your punch stats, performance metrics, and training data stay on your devices. Anonymous usage data (screens viewed, button clicks, app interactions) and a short summary of each workout are sent to Google Analytics to help us improve the app.

Analytics We Use

We use Google Analytics (by Firebase) to see how the app is used, like which screens get opened and how many workouts get done. When you finish a workout, the app sends a short summary with the workout length, punch counts and top speed. It never sends your name, your email, your heart rate, your calories or anything from Apple Health. Google also sees basic device info (phone model, iOS version) and a rough, city level location from your IP address. None of this is linked to you or used for ads.

Kids Can Play Too

PunchWatch is safe for users of all ages. We don't collect any personal information that identifies users.
